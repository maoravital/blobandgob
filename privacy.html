Privacy Policy

Blob & Gob · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Blob & Gob is made by Maor Avital ("we"). It is a game for young children and the whole family.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Blob & Gob has no account or login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not receive any data from your device.

Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

To save your progress, the game stores your level progress, stars, wardrobe choices and settings on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled, the same save data is synced through your own iCloud account (iCloud Key-Value Storage), so your progress follows you between your devices. We cannot see this data.

Purchases

Optional purchases (the Supporter Pack and extra worlds) are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Purchases are behind a parental gate. We receive no payment or card information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

Blob & Gob has no chat, no social features, no ads and no links out of the game for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same link with a new date.
