Blob & Gob: Support

Blob & Gob is a cozy 3D rolling game for kids and families. Drag anywhere to roll a soap bubble and swallow everything smaller than it.

Need help?

Found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at maoravital752@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and tell us what you were doing when it happened.

Progress and purchases

Your progress is saved on your device and in your iCloud. If a level seems stuck or your progress looks wrong, email us and we will help.

Purchases (the Supporter Pack and extra worlds) are handled by Apple. To get them back on a new device, open the purchase screen in the game and tap Restore purchases (a grown-up answers a short question first).

Privacy

We collect no personal data. Read the Privacy Policy.
